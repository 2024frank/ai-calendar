# Code of Conduct

AI Calendar exists to help a community stay connected, and the project should work the same way. We adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as our code of conduct.

In short:

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people new to open source or to the code.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good faith.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks, in public or in private.
- Don't publish anyone's private information without their permission.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer, [@2024frank](https://github.com/2024frank). Reports are handled privately, and every report is reviewed. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
